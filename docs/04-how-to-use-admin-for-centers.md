# How to Use the Center Admin Panel

**For:** EduSphere Center Staff  
**Purpose:** Step-by-step guide to manage your center website  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** June 2026

---

## Welcome

Each EduSphere center has its own website and a **Center Admin Panel** to manage it. From this panel you can:

- Update news, courses, results, and gallery
- View and respond to student enquiries
- Manage directors, faculty, blog, and FAQs
- Control which sections appear on your home page
- Update your center contact details and social links

You do **not** need technical knowledge. If you can use email and upload photos, you can use this panel.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 How to Log In

1. Open your browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox recommended).
2. Go to the **Admin Login** page:  
   `https://[your-domain]/admin/login`
3. Enter your **Username** and **Password** (provided when your center was set up).
4. Click **Sign In**.
5. You will be taken to your **Dashboard**.

> **First-time login:** Change your password immediately (see Section 1.3).

### 1.2 If You Forget Your Password

1. On the login page, click **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter your registered **email address**.
3. Check your email for a reset link.
4. Click the link and set a **new password**.
5. Return to the login page and sign in with your new password.

### 1.3 Change Your Password (Recommended First Step)

1. After login, open the left menu → **My Profile** (under Advanced).
2. Scroll to the **Change Password** section.
3. Enter your **Current Password**.
4. Enter your **New Password** and **Confirm Password**.
5. Click **Update Password**.

> Keep your password private. Do not share it with unauthorized staff.

## 2. Understanding the Admin Panel

### 2.1 Panel Layout

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  TOP BAR: Page Title | Notifications | Dark Mode | Logout  │
├──────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│              │                                               │
│   LEFT       │           MAIN WORK AREA                      │
```

| Area | What It Does |
|------|--------------|
| **Left Menu** | Navigate to all features |
| **Top Bar** | Shows page title, notifications bell, theme toggle, logout |
| **Main Area** | Where you add, edit, and view content |
| **Back Link** | Returns to Dashboard from inner pages |

### 2.2 Left Menu Groups

| Group | Items |
|-------|-------|
| **Main** | Dashboard, Enquiries, Notifications, Activity Logs |
| **Content** | Sections, Home Page Slider, News, Courses, Results, Testimonials, Directors, Senior Faculty, Gallery, Our Centers, Blog, FAQ, Vacancies, About, Contact Page |

### 2.3 Dashboard Overview

When you log in, the **Dashboard** shows:

- **Welcome message** for your center
- **Quick stats** — news count, courses, enquiries, gallery, blog, testimonials
- **Center status** — Active or Inactive
- **Quick action cards** — click any card to go directly to that feature

> Use the Dashboard as your starting point every day.

### 2.4 Notifications

- Click the **bell icon** (top right) to see system messages.
- You will receive notifications when:
  - Your content is **approved** or **rejected** by Head Office
  - Head Office sends a **broadcast message**
  - Important system updates occur

## 3. Daily Tasks

### 3.1 Check New Enquiries (Do This Daily)

Student enquiries come from the **enquiry form** on your website home page.

**Steps:**

1. Go to **Main → Enquiries**.
2. New enquiries appear at the top (unread items are highlighted).
3. Click an enquiry to view full details:
   - Name, phone, email
   - Course interested in
   - Message
   - Date submitted
4. Mark as **Read** after you have followed up.
5. Delete old enquiries once processed (optional).

**Tips:**

- Check enquiries at least **once every morning and evening**.
- Respond to students by phone or email within **24 hours**.
- Use the **search box** to find enquiries by name or course.
- Filter by **Read / Unread** to see pending items only.
- Switch between **List view** and **Card view** using the icons at the top.

## 4. Managing Your Home Page

Your center website home page is made up of **sections** (Hero, News, Courses, Results, etc.). You control what appears on the page.

### 4.1 Enable or Disable Sections

**Steps:**

1. Go to **Content → Sections**.
2. You will see a list of all home page sections.
3. Each section has an **On/Off toggle**.
4. Click the toggle to **enable** (green) or **disable** (grey) a section.
5. Changes apply **immediately** on your public website.

**Example uses:**

- Turn off **Video Testimonials** if you have no videos yet.
- Turn on **Blog** when you start publishing articles.
- Temporarily hide **Results** during off-season.

## 5. Content Management — Step by Step

### 5.1 Home Page Slider (Hero Banner)

**Menu:** Content → Home Page Slider

This is the large banner at the top of your website with the enquiry form.

**To add a new slide:**

1. Click **Add New** (or **+** button).
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** — main heading on the slide
   - **Slider Image** — upload banner image (use **Upload** button)
   - **Button Link** — optional link when user clicks the slide
   - **Display Order** — number to control slide sequence (1 = first)
   - **Active** — keep ON to show the slide
3. Click **Save**.

**Important — Approval Required:**

> New and edited hero slides are sent to **Head Office (Super Admin)** for approval.  
> Until approved, the slide will **not appear** on your public website.  
> Status shows as: **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**.

**Tips:**

- Use clear, high-quality images.
- Keep titles short and action-oriented.
- Maximum image file size: **5 MB**.

---

### 5.2 News & Notifications

**Menu:** Content → News & Notifications

**News** = scrolling updates on the home page (marquee).  
**Notifications** = separate scrolling strip for announcements.

**To add news:**

1. Click **Add New**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** — headline
   - **Content** — full text (rich text editor available)
   - **Link URL** — optional link to more details
   - **Mark as New** — shows a "New" badge
   - **Is Notification** — turn ON if this is a notification (not regular news)
   - **Display Order** — controls order in the marquee
   - **Active** — turn ON to include in the list
3. Click **Save**.

**Important — Approval Required:**

> News items require **Head Office approval** before they appear on your website.  
> Check the **approval status** badge on each item: Pending / Approved / Rejected.

**Bulk actions:**

- Select multiple items using checkboxes.
- Use bulk actions to **activate**, **deactivate**, or **delete** several items at once.

---

### 5.3 Courses

**Menu:** Content → Courses

Manage the course icons shown on your home page (College Level and School Level).

**To add a course:**

1. Click **Add New**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Course Name** — e.g. CAT/MBA, JEE, NEET
   - **Category** — College Level or School Level
   - **Icon** — choose from the icon picker
   - **Display Order** — controls position on the page
   - **Active** — turn ON to show on website
3. Click **Save**.

**Tips:**

- Match course names with EduSphere standard naming.
- Use the correct category so courses appear in the right tab.
- You can also pick courses from the **master course list** if available.

---

### 5.4 Results

**Menu:** Content → Results

Upload achievement and result images for the results slider on your home page.

**To add a result image:**

1. Click **Add New**.
2. Enter a **Title** (e.g. "CAT 2025 Toppers").
3. **Upload image** — required size: **1280 × 720 pixels** exactly.
4. Set **Display Order** and **Active** status.
5. Click **Save**.

**Bulk upload:** Use the bulk upload option to add multiple result images at once.

> Image must be exactly **1280×720 px** and under **5 MB**. Other sizes will be rejected.

---

### 5.5 Testimonials

**Menu:** Content → Testimonials

Manage **text testimonials** (quotes) and **video testimonials** (YouTube links).

**Text testimonial:**

1. Click **Add New** → choose Text.
2. Enter **Quote**, **Author Name**, and **Author Role**.
3. Set display order and save.

**Video testimonial:**

1. Click **Add New** → choose Video.
2. Enter **Title** and **YouTube Video URL** (embed link).
3. Set display order and save.

---

### 5.6 Directors (Leadership)

**Menu:** Content → Directors

Add your center director(s) with photo, bio, and message.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Add New**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Role**, **Photo**, **Bio**.
3. Add **Message Title** and **Message Content** (director's message to students).
4. Set display order — use 1 for primary director.
5. Save.

You can add **multiple directors** for your center.

---

### 5.7 Senior Faculty

**Menu:** Content → Senior Faculty

Showcase your senior faculty members on the home page.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Add New**.
2. Enter **Name**, **Role**, **Description**, and **Photo**.
3. Set display order and active status.
4. Save.

**Bulk upload** is available for adding multiple faculty at once.

---

### 5.8 Gallery

**Menu:** Content → Gallery

Upload photos for the gallery section on your home page.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Add New**.
2. Enter **Title** and **Upload Image** (1280×720 px required).
3. Choose **Gallery Type**: General, Photos, or Videos.
4. Save.

**Bulk upload** is available for multiple images.

---

### 5.9 Blog

**Menu:** Content → Blog

Publish articles, exam tips, and center updates.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Add New**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** and **Slug** (URL-friendly name, auto-generated from title)
   - **Excerpt** — short summary
   - **Content** — full article (rich text editor)
   - **Featured Image** (1280×720 px)
   - **Author Name**
   - **Published Date**
   - **Published** — turn ON to make live
3. Save.

Published posts appear in the **Blog slider** on your home page and have their own detail page.

---

### 5.10 People Also Ask (FAQ)

**Menu:** Content → People Also Ask

Add frequently asked questions for the accordion section on your home page.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Add New**.
2. Enter **Question** and **Answer**.
3. Set display order and save.

**Bulk upload** is available for importing many FAQs at once.

---

### 5.11 Vacancies

**Menu:** Content → Vacancies

Post job openings for your center. These appear on `/{your-center}/vacancies`.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Add New**.
2. Enter job **Title**, **Description**, and details.
3. Set **Active** to show on the vacancies page.
4. Save.

Students can apply via **WhatsApp** from the vacancies page.

---

### 5.12 Get To Know Us (About)

**Menu:** Content → Get To Know Us

Edit the "About" section on your home page.

**Steps:**

1. Open the page — usually one record per center.
2. Update **Heading**, **Description**, **Subtext**.
3. Add **Feature List** items (bullet points about your center).
4. Upload an **About Image**.
5. Save.

---

### 5.13 Contact Page

**Menu:** Content → Contact Page

Customize the heading and intro text for the contact area on your home page.

**Steps:**

1. Open the page.
2. Edit **Heading** and **Intro Text**.
3. Save.

> Actual contact details (phone, email, address) are managed in **Settings** (see Section 6).

---

### 5.14 Our Centers

**Menu:** Content → Our Centers

View and edit how nearby EduSphere centers are displayed in your "Our Centers" section.

> **Note:** Which centers appear in your list is configured by **Head Office**. You can update display information for centers already assigned to you.

## 6. Center Settings

**Menu:** Advanced → Settings

Update your center's core information. Changes reflect in the **header, footer, and contact sections** of your website.

| Field | Where It Appears |
|-------|------------------|
| Center Name | Header, footer, page titles |
| Description | Meta description, about areas |
| Email | Footer, contact section |
| Phone Numbers | Footer, contact section (you can add multiple) |
| Address | Footer, contact section, map |
| Working Hours | Contact section |
| Google Rating & URL | Contact section |
| Map Embed URL | Google Maps on contact section |
| Logo (Light / Dark) | Website header |
| Favicon | Browser tab icon |
| Social Links | Header and footer (Facebook, X, Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram) |

**Steps:**

1. Go to **Advanced → Settings**.
2. Update the fields you need.
3. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom.

> Keep phone numbers and email **up to date** — students use these to contact you.

## 7. Approval Process (Important)

Some content must be **approved by Head Office** before it goes live on your public website.

| Content | Needs Approval? | Goes Live When |
|---------|-----------------|----------------|
| News items | **Yes** | Head Office approves |
| Hero slider slides | **Yes** | Head Office approves |
| Courses | No | Immediately after save |
| Results | No | Immediately after save |
| Gallery | No | Immediately after save |
| Blog | No | When you set Published = ON |
| Testimonials | No | Immediately after save |
| Directors / Faculty | No | Immediately after save |
| FAQ | No | Immediately after save |
| Vacancies | No | Immediately after save |
| Settings | No | Immediately after save |

**What to do when content is Pending:**

1. Wait for Head Office to review (usually within 1–2 working days).
2. Check **Notifications** (bell icon) for approval or rejection message.
3. If **Rejected**, edit the content and save again to resubmit.

**Status badges you will see:**

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Pending** | Waiting for Head Office approval |
| **Approved** | Live on your website |
| **Rejected** | Not approved — edit and resubmit |

## 8. Other Useful Features

### 8.1 Activity Logs

**Menu:** Main → Activity Logs

View a history of all changes made in your admin panel — who changed what and when. Useful for tracking updates across your team.

### 8.2 Content Versions

**Menu:** Advanced → Content Versions

View previous versions of content and **restore** an older version if needed.

### 8.3 Center Admins

**Menu:** Advanced → Center Admins

Add or manage admin accounts for your center team (if you are the primary admin).

**Steps to add a team member:**

1. Click **Add New**.
2. Enter **Username**, **Email**, **Full Name**, and **Password**.
3. Assign a **Role** (Admin, Editor, or Viewer).
4. Save.

| Role | Access Level |
|------|--------------|
| **Admin** | Full access — create, edit, delete |
| **Editor** | Create and edit — cannot delete |
| **Viewer** | Read only — cannot make changes |

### 8.4 Roles & Permissions

**Menu:** Advanced → Roles & Permissions

Create custom roles or adjust permissions for your team members.

## 9. Image Upload Guidelines

Follow these rules to avoid upload errors:

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Maximum file size** | 5 MB |
| **Gallery, Results, Blog, Centers images** | Exactly **1280 × 720 pixels** |
| **Formats** | JPG, PNG, WebP (recommended) |
| **Hero / Director / Faculty photos** | High quality; under 5 MB |
| **File names** | Use simple names without special characters |

**Tips for good photos:**

- Use well-lit, professional images.
- Avoid blurry or low-resolution photos.
- Crop images to the required size **before** uploading.
- Compress large files using free tools (e.g. TinyPNG) if over 5 MB.

## 11. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Cannot log in | Check username/password. Use **Forgot Password**. Contact Head Office if still blocked. |
| "Please login again" message | Session expired. Log in again. |
| Image upload fails | Check file size (max 5 MB) and dimensions (1280×720 for gallery/results/blog). |
| News/Hero not on website | Check approval status — must be **Approved** by Head Office. |
| Section not visible on website | Go to **Sections** and ensure the section is **enabled (ON)**. |
| Changes not showing | Refresh browser (Ctrl+F5). Wait 1–2 minutes for cache to update. |
| Cannot access another center's admin | Each admin account is tied to one center only — this is normal. |
